Please read below before asking for someone to be banned form the site:

"Nothing can be loved or hated unless it is first understood." - Leonardo DaVinci

Everyone is here for a reason. People come for friendship, laughs, sex, attention, or money, some come for the drama, and for some it's their only window to the outside world. Whatever your reason for being here, our job is to deliver the experience you came for.

There are only 3 instances when we will always ban an account:

  • Someone is suspected of being underage.
  • Someone is attempting to defraud.
  • Someone is breaking a common law.

In most other cases we will do everything in our power not to ban, but work to find a happy medium where each person can get what they came for without ruining the experience for anyone else. This means that if someone is being rude to you and your conflict can't be resolved, we kindly ask that you click the Block button in their user menu to never see or hear them again. If that fails, please report them to a moderator on the site before filing an abuse complaint.

Content Complaint Resolution Policy

Our policy is to review and resolve all reported content complaints within five (5) business days. In the event there is evidence of illegal content, it will be removed immediately. In the event the complainant disagrees with our decision, the disagreement shall be resolved by a neutral body, at our expense.
